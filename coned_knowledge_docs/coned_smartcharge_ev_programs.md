# Consolidated Edison: Electric Vehicles & SmartCharge New York

## Overview
As part of Con Edison's Clean Energy Commitment, the company is supporting the electrification of transportation across New York City and Westchester County. Con Edison offers managed charging programs and infrastructure incentives for individual drivers, commercial fleets, and charging network operators.

## SmartCharge New York (Residential & Light-Duty Drivers)
SmartCharge New York is Con Edison's flagship managed charging program that rewards electric vehicle (EV) drivers for charging at times that support grid reliability.

### Key Financial Rewards & Incentives:
- **Annual Earnings**: Participating drivers earn an average of **$400 to $500 per year** in cash rewards.
- **Off-Peak Night Charging**: Earn **10 cents per kilowatt-hour (kWh)** for charging during off-peak overnight hours between **midnight and 8:00 AM EST** (year-round).
- **Peak Avoidance Bonus**: Earn rewards for avoiding charging during high-demand summer peak hours (weekdays 2:00 PM to 6:00 PM, June through September).
- **Onboarding Milestone**: $25 bonus after the first three months of active data recording.

### Compatible Technology:
- Integrated with vehicle telematics (Tesla, Ford, Hyundai, Chevrolet, Nissan, etc.) and connected Level 2 smart home chargers (ChargePoint, Enel X Way, Wallbox).

## PowerReady EV Program (Make-Ready Infrastructure)
- **$500+ Million Investment**: One of the largest utility-administered EV infrastructure incentive programs in the United States.
- **Coverage**: Covers up to 100% of the electrical grid infrastructure costs (transformers, service panels, trenching, conduit) required to install Level 2 and DC Fast Chargers (DCFC) for businesses, public parking lots, multi-unit dwellings, and commercial fleet depots.
- **Disadvantaged Communities Focus**: Higher incentive tiers (up to 100% funding) for projects installed in designated environmental justice communities.

## Fleet Electrification & Idling Reduction
- **Municipal & Commercial Fleet Advisory**: Dedicated Con Edison engineering advisory for NYC municipal and commercial fleets transitioning from diesel to medium- and heavy-duty battery electric trucks.
- **Telematics & Anti-Idling**: Supporting smart telematics and battery auxiliary power units to eliminate engine idling during utility field work and construction.
