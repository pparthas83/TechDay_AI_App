Consolidated Edison: Company Profile & Operating Territories

Overview & Heritage

Consolidated Edison, Inc. (NYSE: ED) is one of the nation's largest investor-owned energy delivery companies, tracing its origins back to 1823 as the New York Gas Light Company and later Thomas Edison's Edison Electric Illuminating Company of New York in 1882.

Operating Subsidiaries

1. **Consolidated Edison Company of New York, Inc. (CECONY)**:

- Provides electric service to approximately 3.6 million customers in Manhattan, Brooklyn, Queens, Bronx, and Staten Island, as well as Westchester County.

- Provides natural gas service to 1.1 million customers in Manhattan, the Bronx, parts of Queens, and Westchester County.

- Operates the largest commercial steam system in the world, delivering high-pressure steam for heating and cooling iconic Manhattan high-rises including the Empire State Building, Chrysler Building, and Grand Central Terminal.

2. **Orange and Rockland Utilities, Inc. (O&R)**:

- Delivers electricity and natural gas to customers in southeastern New York state (Rockland, Orange, and Sullivan counties) and northern New Jersey (via Rockland Electric Company).

Key Scale & Reliability Statistics

  • **Population Served**: Over 10 million people across 660 square miles of urban and suburban territory.
  • **Electric Reliability**: Con Edison's system reliability consistently exceeds 99.99%, ranking among the highest electric reliability indices (SAIFI and CAIDI) in the United States, driven by its redundant second-contingency underground network design.
  • **Clean Energy Goals**: Committed to delivering 100% clean, net-zero greenhouse gas emissions energy to customers by 2050.
