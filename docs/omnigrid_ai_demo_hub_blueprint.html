Executive Summary

OmniGrid AI is a reference architecture and live working platform designed to bridge the chasm between Operational Technology (OT) (SCADA, Outage Management Systems, Wholesale Grid Markets) and Customer Experience (CX) (Contact Centers, Web Portals, Field Safety) for electric and gas utilities.

Originally piloted as the keynote centerpiece for Con Edison's Tech Day: AI in Action, the platform deploys an interactive 3D holographic agent ("Watt") backed by a dual-core agentic engine (Google Dialogflow CX Playbooks + Gemini 3.6 Flash). Unlike conventional chatbots that rely on static FAQ retrieval, OmniGrid AI autonomously orchestrates real-time external telemetry feeds, deterministic engineering calculators, and regulatory frameworks—synthesizing spoken, photorealistic audio-visual responses in under 1.5 seconds.

This document outlines the strategy to generalize this prototype into a turnkey, white-label Google Cloud Demo Hub asset applicable to any electric, gas, or dual-fuel utility worldwide.

1. Core Value Proposition: Why Utilities Need OmniGrid AI

  • Electrification & Grid Capacity Crunches: Rapid adoption of EV chargers, data centers, and heat pumps is stressing distribution substations. Utilities need autonomous systems to communicate grid stress and guide time-of-use adoption.
  • Extreme Weather & Wildfire Resilience: Increasing storm frequency demands sub-second, transparent communication on restorations and reliability without overwhelming call center queues.
  • Decarbonization Mandates: Property owners and contractors are overwhelmed by overlapping federal (IRA 25C/179D), state, and municipal carbon laws (NYC Local Law 97, Boston BERDO).
  • Call Center Inflation: Contact center handling costs exceed –2 per call. OmniGrid AI provides instant conversational clarity while enforcing utility engineering standards.

4. The Four High-Impact Utility Use Cases

Pillar 1: Front-Office Customer Engagement & Bill Explainer

Translates 15-minute AMI smart meter interval spikes into spoken, plain-language explanations. Solves summer and winter bill shock and defers high-cost Tier-1 contact center volume.

Pillar 2: Real-Time Grid Resilience & Storm Situational Awareness

Correlates live OMS restoration numbers with NOAA storm warnings and wholesale RTO generation margins in under 1.5 seconds. Gives executives, media, and public customers immediate transparency during weather emergencies.

Pillar 3: Decarbonization & Clean Heat Acceleration

Instantly calculates cold-climate heat pump sizing, prescriptive utility rebates, Disadvantaged Communities (DAC) equity bonuses, and building emissions penalty avoidance. Natively enforces mandatory ACCA Manual J engineering disclaimers.

Pillar 4: Field Operations Safety & IoT Asset Monitoring

Provides conversational access to subsurface acoustic IoT sensors (manhole safety) and telematics (fleet vehicle idling reduction), directly targeting utility worker safety and corporate Scope 1 emission goals.
