# Consolidated Edison: Subsurface Engineering & Underground Safety

## Underground Electric Distribution Network
Con Edison operates the largest underground electric network in the world, with over 100,000 miles of underground cables, 260,000 manholes, and underground vaults crisscrossing the five boroughs of New York City and parts of Westchester.

## Manhole Acoustic Monitoring & Machine Learning
- **Edge Acoustic Sensors**: Ruggedized, waterproof acoustic sensors deployed inside underground electric vaults to capture high-frequency sonic signatures.
- **Micro-Arcing Detection**: As secondary low-voltage cables and lead-sheathed cables age or endure salt-water winter slush intrusion, insulation breakdown produces distinct pre-failure acoustic signals (micro-arcing, buzzing, and partial discharges).
- **Edge AI & Predictive Alerts**: Machine learning algorithms classify acoustic signatures in real time, separating background traffic rumble and subway vibrations from genuine electrical cable breakdown.
- **Proactive Remediation**: Sends automated alerts to dispatch crews to de-energize and repair compromised conductors days or weeks before smoke, fire, or manhole displacement occurs.

## Physical Safety Innovations
- **Vented Manhole Covers**: Engineered composite and slotted cast-iron covers that allow gases to safely dissipate while preventing high pressure accumulation.
- **Infrared Thermal Inspections**: Mobile thermal imaging vans and handheld FLIR cameras scan thousands of street covers annually to detect subterranean heat buildup.
- **Atmospheric Gas Detection**: Sniffer trucks equipped with laser spectroscopy continuously patrol city streets detecting stray voltage and volatile gas concentrations.
