Consolidated Edison: Clean Energy Commitment & Clean Heat Programs

Overview & Vision

Consolidated Edison, Inc. (Con Edison) is dedicated to achieving the goals of New York State's Climate Leadership and Community Protection Act (CLCPA), which targets 70% renewable electricity by 2030 and a zero-emission electric grid by 2040. Con Edison's Clean Energy Commitment focuses on building electrification, renewable integration, energy storage, and modernizing the electric transmission and distribution system across New York City and Westchester County.

Clean Heat Program & Heat Pump Incentives

Con Edison, in collaboration with the statewide NY Clean Heat program, offers substantial financial incentives and rebates to residential, multifamily, and commercial customers transitioning from fossil-fuel heating (oil, propane, or natural gas) to clean, electric heating and cooling systems.

1. Air-Source Heat Pumps (ASHP)

  • **Residential Systems**: Whole-home and partial-home heat pump installations qualify for thousands of dollars in rebates depending on heating capacity and efficiency (SEER2 and HSPF2 ratings).
  • **Functionality**: ASHPs extract heat from outdoor air even in freezing winter temperatures and transfer it inside, while reversing in summer to provide high-efficiency air conditioning.
  • **Contractor Network**: Rebates must be processed through Con Edison Participating Clean Heat Contractors.

    • 2. Geothermal (Ground-Source) Heat Pumps (GSHP)

  • **High-Efficiency Ground Loops**: Ground-source heat pumps tap into constant underground temperatures to provide maximum seasonal coefficient of performance (COP).
  • **Incentive Levels**: Up to $30,000 for single-family residential homes.
  • **Disadvantaged Communities (DAC) Bonus**: Properties located in state-designated Disadvantaged Communities are eligible for enhanced rebates of up to $40,000.

    • 3. Heat Pump Water Heaters (HPWH)

  • Instant point-of-sale rebates up to $1,000 for ENERGY STAR® certified hybrid electric heat pump water heaters, reducing water heating energy usage by up to 70%.

    • 4. Commercial and Multifamily Clean Heat

  • Comprehensive custom incentives for multifamily buildings (5+ units) and commercial facilities, covering technical feasibility studies, electrical service panel upgrades, and central heat pump system installations.
  • Focus on reducing building greenhouse gas emissions in compliance with New York City Local Law 97 (LL97).

    • Energy Efficiency & Weatherization Rebates

  • **Smart Thermostat Rebates**: Instant $50 to $85 discounts on qualified Google Nest and ecobee smart thermostats.
  • **Insulation & Air Sealing**: Up to 70% to 100% discount on comprehensive air sealing and attic insulation upgrades for income-eligible households.
  • **Appliance Rebates**: Rebates for ENERGY STAR® certified refrigerators, dehumidifiers, and room air conditioners.
