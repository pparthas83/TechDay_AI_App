Consolidated Edison: Customer Billing, Rates & Financial Assistance

Advanced Metering Infrastructure (AMI) & Smart Meters

  • **Smart Meter Deployment**: Over 5 million smart electric meters and advanced gas modules deployed across Manhattan, Brooklyn, Queens, Bronx, Staten Island, and Westchester County.
  • **Interval Data**: Smart meters record electricity consumption in 15-minute intervals and gas consumption in hourly intervals.
  • **Customer Insights**: Through the My Account portal, customers can visualize hour-by-hour energy usage, identify high-consumption appliances, set customized bill alerts, and analyze weather-driven usage spikes.

    • Customer Assistance & Payment Programs

    Con Edison offers comprehensive payment relief and assistance programs to support customers facing financial hardship:

    1. Energy Affordability Program (EAP) / Low-Income Discount

  • Monthly bill discounts automatically provided to customers enrolled in qualifying government assistance programs, including:

    • - Supplemental Nutrition Assistance Program (SNAP)

    - Home Energy Assistance Program (HEAP)

    - Supplemental Security Income (SSI)

    - Medicaid

    - Temporary Assistance for Needy Families (TANF)

  • Monthly discounts range from $20 to over $150 depending on service type (electric-only, gas-heating, non-heating electric).

    • 2. Deferred Payment Agreements (DPA)

  • Flexible installment payment plans allowing residential and small business customers to pay past-due balances over time with $0 down payment options and terms extending up to 24 months.

    • 3. Level Payment / Budget Billing

  • Evens out seasonal bill volatility (such as summer AC cooling costs or winter heating spikes) by averaging annual energy costs across 12 equal monthly installments.

    • Time-of-Use (TOU) Rates

  • **Voluntary TOU Rate Plans**: Electric rates vary based on the time of day energy is consumed.
  • **Off-Peak Savings**: Lower delivery charges during overnight and weekend periods; higher rates during peak weekday afternoon hours (2:00 PM to 6:00 PM).
  • **Target Customers**: Especially advantageous for EV owners, heat pump owners, and customers capable of shifting discretionary washer/dryer/dishwasher usage to overnight windows.
